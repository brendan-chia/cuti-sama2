# Moderated usability script — five-minute preference round

## Goal

Confirm that a participant can understand and submit all five preference cards in under five minutes without drag gestures or facilitator help.

## Setup

- Four connected clients in a trip with locked constraints.
- One participant uses the screen reader and system dynamic text; one repeats with Reduce Motion enabled.
- Start the timer when the Vibe card becomes visible.

## Script

1. Ask: “Without touching the screen, tell me what you think happens here.” Record whether blind submission is understood.
2. Ask the participant to submit Vibe and Pace using taps only. Record time, mis-taps, and whether examples help.
3. Ask them to submit Must-have, Nice-to-have, and Avoid / dealbreaker without facilitator hints.
4. Before a reveal, ask them to change one submitted card. After reveal, ask whether they expect it to remain editable.
5. Enable the assigned accessibility mode and repeat one round. Confirm labels are announced fully, content is not clipped, touch targets are comfortable, and reduced-motion transitions contain no throws or flips.
6. Stop the timer when the fifth card reports Ready.

## Pass criteria

- Median completion time is at most 5:00; every participant completes without drag.
- No hidden peer card is discovered before reveal.
- At least 4/5 participants correctly predict edit-before-reveal and read-only-after-reveal behavior.
- No critical screen-reader, dynamic-text, touch-target, or motion issue is observed.
